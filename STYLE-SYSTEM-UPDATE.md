# Version D: section dividers and contact hierarchy

This update supersedes the earlier style guide's restriction on profile-inspired stripes and its descriptions of the older monochrome layout.

## Section dividers

Use the vivid colours from the original social profile image as thin section dividers:

- Cyan #00dfe5 before Mark's introduction.
- Pink #f38aaf before services.
- Lime #a5ee24 before illustrated everyday situations.
- Magenta #f000ca before contact.

Each divider has three unequal segments, the final two progressively lighter. Height: 6 px desktop, 5 px mobile. Leave generous whitespace. Colour is decorative; every section still has a clear heading. Keep stripes away from text and QR modules.

Retain navy #152238, warm white #fffefa, cream backgrounds, pastel speech bubbles, Oswald headings and Poppins body text. The gentleman and scenario images use the established painted style. The character is not a portrait; the separate original social-profile image helps visitors recognise Mark's accounts.

## Service and contact layout

Keep the five hero bubbles aligned in two columns, with the final bubble spanning both. Use a single column on very narrow screens. Four service disclosures open in place.

Put Email Mark and Text Mark now before the optional email composer. The composer opens a draft for the visitor to review and send; it does not submit to a backend. Preserve direct contact details and their copy controls.

The service inventory now appears as a checklist between the large LinkedIn/Instagram cards and the thank-you message. Do not duplicate it as another main-page section.

WhatsApp remains pending confirmation that Mark's number is registered. Once confirmed, add a direct click-to-chat link alongside email/SMS. A click opens the conversation; the visitor still sends the message, and response timing is not guaranteed.

Keep the existing version D URL and printed QR destinations unchanged.
